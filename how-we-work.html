How We Work

The terms that apply to every engagement

Get in touch

Fees depend on the programme and are agreed in writing before work starts.

Insurance

We hold professional indemnity, public liability and employers' liability insurance. Limits and certificates are provided on request. See also our credentials.

Response times

  • Enquiries are acknowledged within one working day.
  • A written proposal follows within two working days of the scope being agreed.
  • NEC4 programme reviews are delivered inside the reply period at clause 31.3, provided the file reaches us with at least five working days remaining.
  • During an engagement, correspondence is answered within one working day.

Confidentiality and data protection

  • We sign client non disclosure agreements as a matter of course, and we do not name clients or projects publicly without written permission.
  • Cyber Essentials certified, whole organisation, and registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • Project data is held only for as long as the engagement and our record keeping obligations require, and is returned or destroyed on request. See our privacy policy.
  • Where a client requires data to remain on their own systems, we work inside their environment rather than taking copies.

Raising a concern

If something is wrong, tell us directly and the director will deal with it. Concerns can also be raised anonymously through our human rights policy.
