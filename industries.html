Industries

Where our programme work is done, and what changes from one sector to the next.

Sectors

Electricity networks

Substation, overhead line, cabling and civils work for transmission and distribution network owners, mostly under NEC4. We prepare tender programmes, NEC programme submissions, impacted programmes for compensation events and monthly updates for contractors and their supply chains. See related case studies.

Gas networks

Programme and Primavera work for gas transmission and distribution capital delivery.

Water

AMP8 delivery under frameworks, where the programme is measured against regulatory dates as well as its own logic.

Highways and civils

NEC programme submission, acceptance and governance for road and civils schemes.

Rail

Possession-led programmes and P6 support for renewals and enhancements.

Defence

Programme and project controls support for defence infrastructure and capital programmes, where approval gateways, earned value reporting and controlled change shape how the programme is built and governed.
