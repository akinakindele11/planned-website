Tendering for work?

Most tenders ask for a programme with the price, often at short notice.

We build it in Primavera P6 to the client's requirements, with a short narrative that explains the sequence and the key dates. It gives the assessors a programme they can believe, and if you win it becomes the starting point for the contract programme.

After award we can take it through to acceptance and keep it updated each month, or hand it over to your own team.
