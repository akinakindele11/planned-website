Primavera P6 and Primavera Cloud administration

An environment your planners can trust: the right structure, the right access, and data that means the same thing on every project.

Talk to us

Most problems with enterprise P6 are not software faults. They come from structure and data: an EPS that no longer matches the organisation, access that is too wide or too narrow, global codes and calendars that every planner has added to, and layouts nobody owns. Reporting suffers first, then trust in the system.

Planned administers Primavera P6 EPPM, P6 Professional and Oracle Primavera Cloud for owners, contractors and programme teams, as a defined project or as ongoing support. Our case studies include database consolidation and enterprise implementations for transmission and gas distribution networks.

What we look after

  • Structure: the enterprise project structure, organisational breakdown and portfolios, aligned to how the organisation is run and reported.
  • Security: users, security profiles and access, including separating access between business units, contracts or suppliers where that is required.
  • Global data: activity and project codes, calendars, user-defined fields and resource dictionaries, with rules on who can create what.
  • Standards: templates, layouts and reporting that make every project comparable.
  • Housekeeping: baselines, archiving and data quality checks, so the database stays usable.
  • Upgrades and integration: planning and testing upgrades with your IT team, and supporting interfaces with cost and reporting systems.

Taking over an environment

We start with an audit: structure, security, global data, standards and housekeeping, with findings ranked by what they cost you in reporting and risk. You get a plan to fix them in an order that does not disrupt live projects, and we then carry it out or support your team to.

Consolidation and migration

We consolidate multiple P6 databases into one, restructure the EPS and access model, and migrate between P6 versions or from P6 to Oracle Primavera Cloud. Each migration is rehearsed and checked against the source before cut-over. For a move to the cloud, see our migration readiness assessment and Primavera Cloud consultancy.

Ways to engage

  • Project: an audit, a consolidation, a restructure or an upgrade, scoped and priced in writing.
  • Ongoing support: a named administrator from our team for a set number of days a month, with agreed response times.
  • Cover: support while your own administrator is away or the role is being filled.

Common Questions

Do you work on P6 EPPM, P6 Professional and Primavera Cloud?

Yes, all three.

Do you sell Oracle licences?

No. We help you choose the right licences and set them up.

Can you separate access between business units or suppliers?

Yes. We design the structure and security profiles so each group sees and edits only what it should, and test it before it goes live.

Do you work with our IT team on upgrades?

Yes. Upgrades and infrastructure changes are planned and tested with your IT team, who keep control of your servers or tenancy.

Can you support us on an ongoing basis?

Yes. A named administrator from our team is available for an agreed number of days a month, with response times set out in writing.
