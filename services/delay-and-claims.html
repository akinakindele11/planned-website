Delay analysis and extension of time

What the delay was, what caused it and what time it is worth, set out so it holds up when it is tested.

Talk to us

Time claims are usually won or lost on records rather than argument. We carry out delay analysis for contractors, clients and their advisers under NEC, JCT and FIDIC, from a single compensation event to a full extension of time claim or defence.

What we do

  • Compensation event time assessment under NEC: the effect on the Accepted Programme, ready to support a quotation.
  • Extension of time claims: the delays, their causes and their effect on completion, with the evidence a JCT or FIDIC claim needs.
  • Responding to claims: a review of a contractor's time claim or quotation for clients and project managers.
  • Programme records: getting the as-built record and updates into shape before a claim is needed.
  • Support to advisers: programme analysis for commercial teams and legal advisers.

Choosing the method

The right method depends on the contract, the question being asked and the records available: impacted as-planned, time impact analysis, windows analysis, as-planned versus as-built or collapsed as-built. We agree the method with you before the analysis starts. See our guide to delay analysis methods.

Where to start

If you are not yet sure the position is worth pursuing, start with a Delay and EOT Position Review: a fixed-scope check of what your records support before you commission a full analysis.

Common Questions

Do you act for contractors or clients?

Both. We assess delay for contractors claiming time and review claims for clients and project managers. Where an existing relationship would compromise our independence, we say so and decline.

Which contracts do you work under?

NEC4 and NEC3, JCT and FIDIC. The analysis follows what the contract says about time, programmes and notices.

What if our programme records are poor?

We tell you what can still be shown and what cannot. A Delay and EOT Position Review is designed to answer that before you spend on a full analysis.

Project running late?

Send us a short note on the contract, what has caused the delay and the records you have. We will come back within one working day with how we can help.
