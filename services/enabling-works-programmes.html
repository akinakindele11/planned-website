Programmes for enabling and early works

Utility diversions, site clearance, demolition, earthworks and temporary works, planned in Primavera P6 to your client's requirements and kept current until the site is handed on.

Talk to us

Enabling works go first, so their programme is usually the first one the client tests and the one every later package depends on. It also has to carry the scheme's unknowns: services that are not where the drawings say, access and possessions that arrive late, permits and outages that move, and ground that is not what the survey suggested.

We plan enabling and early works in Primavera P6, to the programme requirements your client sets, and keep the programme current so that when something changes you can show its effect on time quickly and with the records to back it.

What we plan

  • Utility diversions and protection: statutory undertaker design and approval, outages and shutdowns, permits and the tie-ins that release the next package.
  • Site clearance and demolition: surveys, consents, ecology windows and the sequence that hands sites over on time.
  • Earthworks, drainage and groundworks: seasonal working, haul routes and the interfaces with the main works.
  • Temporary works and access: traffic management, possessions and the temporary arrangements the rest of the scheme relies on.

What we do

  • Tender programmes: a credible P6 programme with a short narrative, often at short notice. See tender programmes.
  • First programme for acceptance: built to your client's specification and, under NEC, to what clause 31 requires. See NEC programmes and monthly updates.
  • Monthly or fortnightly updates: progress recorded against what happened on site, with a narrative your client can accept.
  • Compensation events and delay: impacted programmes when access is late, services are found or information changes, linked to the work they affected. See delay and claims.

Why records matter most here

Enabling works absorb more change than any other package, and the time you are entitled to depends on what your programme and records show at the moment the change happens. A programme that is updated each period, with progress recorded against the work actually done, is what turns an unrecorded service or a late possession into time rather than an argument. We set the programme up so that evidence builds as the work goes on.

How engagements usually run

Most clients start with a tender programme or a first programme for acceptance, then keep us on for the monthly updates and any compensation events. Others call us in when a programme has been rejected or a delay needs to be assessed. Scope and fee are agreed in writing before any work starts. See how we work.

Common Questions

Can you work to our client's programme specification?

Yes. We build to the specification and procedure your client issues, including their WBS, coding, calendars and reporting, and issue native P6 files and PDF.

Do we need our own Primavera P6 licence?

No. We can host your programme in our own licensed Primavera P6 and issue the native files and PDFs your client asks for.

How quickly can you turn round a tender programme?

Usually within days. Send the tender documents and the submission date and we will confirm scope and delivery within one working day.

Do you work under NEC?

Yes, under NEC4 and NEC3, and under JCT. The programme follows what the contract requires for content, acceptance and revisions.

Can you help when a programme has been rejected?

Yes. We review the reasons given, put the programme right and resubmit it, and can support the response to your client.

Starting a package?

Send us the scope, your client's programme requirements and the dates you are working to. We will come back within one working day.
