Your programme, accepted and kept current

We build your first programme for acceptance, then keep it up to date at the frequency the contract asks for, fortnightly or monthly.

Request a proposal

After award the clock starts quickly. Under the NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract, if no programme is identified in the Contract Data, the first one has to be submitted within the period stated there, and a quarter of the Price for Work Done to Date is retained until it is submitted with the information the contract requires.

Planned builds that first programme in Primavera P6, takes it through to acceptance, and then keeps it current, so your programme stays a tool you can use rather than a document you have to defend.

First programme

  • Logic-linked and resourced as required, with the clause 31 content the Project Manager will look for: dates, float, time risk allowances, order and timing of work, and the information the Scope asks for.
  • Under a Term Service Contract, the plan and any Task Order programmes, set up so they can be updated across the term.

Updates

  • Progress recorded against site information, with a short narrative on what moved and why.
  • Early warning of slippage, so problems are raised while they can still be managed.
  • Compensation event time effects assessed where instructed, using the Accepted Programme as the base.

Format and delivery

Primavera P6 native files and PDF, in the client's format. We hold our own P6 licence, so you do not need one. Delivery is remote, with site visits as needed.

Commercials

A day rate or a fixed fee per update, agreed in writing before we start. Tell us the contract, the start date and the update frequency, and we will send a proposal.

Common Questions

What does the Project Manager look for in a first programme under NEC4?

The information listed in clause 31.2, including the starting date, access dates, key dates, the order and timing of the work, float, time risk allowances and the information the Scope requires. A programme missing any of these can be rejected.

How often do you update the programme?

At the frequency the contract asks for. Under the ECC that is usually monthly or as stated in the Contract Data; some clients ask for fortnightly updates.

Can you work under the Term Service Contract?

Yes. Under the TSC the document is the plan rather than the programme, and work often arrives by Task Order. We set up the plan and any Task Order programmes so they can be updated over the term.

Do we need our own Primavera P6 licence?

No. We work in our own licensed Primavera P6 and issue native files and PDFs.

Do you assess compensation events?

Yes, where instructed. We assess the time effect of compensation events using the Accepted Programme as the base, so the quotation is supported by the programme.
