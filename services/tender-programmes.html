Tender programmes, built to win and ready to deliver

Your bid programme is scored, then it becomes the programme you are held to. We build it so it does both.

Send us your ITT

Most tenders and framework call-offs ask for a programme with the price, often with a short turnaround. The programme is scored, and if you win, it becomes the starting point for the programme you are held to.

Planned builds tender programmes in Primavera P6, or your own tool, to the ITT requirements and the client's specification. You submit a programme that reads as credible to the evaluator and can be defended at interview.

What you get

  • A logic-linked tender programme in Primavera P6 (or your tool), built to the ITT requirements and the client's WBS, calendar and coding rules.
  • A short programme narrative covering sequence, key dates, access, phasing, interfaces and risk.
  • Critical path and float checked before issue, so the evaluator does not find open ends, constraints doing the work or a completion that only holds on paper.
  • Native files plus PDF in the format the client asks for.

How it works

Send the ITT pack, the key dates and your method or resource assumptions. We confirm the scope and the delivery date the same working day, build the programme, and walk your bid team through it before submission so you can answer questions on it at interview.

Quick turnaround is available when the deadline is close.

Framework call-offs

Winning a place on a framework is the first step. Most mini-competitions then ask for a programme with each price. If your team is stretched at tender stage, we can build the programme for each call-off to the same standard, so every submission reads the same way.

Under NEC

The tender programme is set up so it can become your first programme for acceptance after award, with the clause 31 content in place from the start. After award we can carry on with the first submission and the monthly updates, or hand the programme over to your team.

Common Questions

What do you need from us to build a tender programme?

The ITT pack or the client's programme requirements, the key dates, and your method statement or resource assumptions. Where the method is not settled yet, we agree the assumptions with your bid team and state them in the narrative.

Can you work to a short tender deadline?

Yes. We confirm the scope and the delivery date the same working day, and quick turnaround is available when the deadline is close.

Do we need our own Primavera P6 licence?

No. We build in our own licensed Primavera P6 and issue native files and PDFs. We can also work in the tool the client specifies.

Can the tender programme become the NEC first programme?

Yes. We set it up with the clause 31 content in place from the start, so after award it can be developed into the first programme you submit for acceptance.

Do you support framework call-offs as well as one-off bids?

Yes. We prepare programmes for individual call-offs and mini-competitions under frameworks, as well as for single tenders.

Tender deadline coming up?

Send us the ITT and the submission date. We will confirm scope and timing the same working day.
