Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

General

What services does Planned Limited offer?

+

Five service lines for construction and infrastructure: planning and scheduling, project controls and PMO support, delay and claims, schedule assurance and reviews, and Primavera P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud systems and training. See all services.

What industries do you work in?

+

Mainly UK infrastructure: electricity transmission and distribution, gas networks, water, highways and civils, and rail, plus programmes in the Gulf under FIDIC. See Industries.

Services & Process

What is your typical project duration?

+

It depends on the job. A tender programme or a programme review is a short, fixed-scope piece of work. Monthly updates and interim planning run for as long as you need them. We agree the timetable in writing before work starts.

Tools & Technical

What versions of Primavera P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud do you support?

+

We work with current releases of Primavera P6 Professional, P6 EPPM and Oracle Primavera Cloud, and can advise on upgrades and on moving between them.

Do you provide on-site support?

+

Yes, we can provide on-site support for embedded consultancy roles, training delivery, and implementation projects. We also offer remote support and advisory services for maximum flexibility.

How do you ensure quality in your deliverables?

+

Every programme is checked before issue for logic, critical path, float and constraints, and every deliverable is reviewed by a senior planner before it is issued, including work by associate planners.

Do you work remotely, or only on site?

+

Both. Most of our planning, project controls and P6 work is delivered remotely across the UK, which keeps things flexible and cost-effective. Where a project genuinely needs someone in the room, for a workshop, a mobilisation or a difficult recovery, we come on site.

Which project planning tools do you use?

+

We work primarily in Primavera P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud, and we use Microsoft Project where a client's environment requires it. If you are moving between these tools, or standardising on one, we can advise on the right fit and handle the migration.

Do you offer a free schedule check?

+

Yes. You can run your own Primavera P6 schedule through our free online P6 health check. Upload an .xer or .xml file and it scores the programme against recognised DCMA-style quality metrics right in your browser, with nothing uploaded to a server. It is a quick way to see where a schedule stands before deciding whether you need a hand.

Costs & Commercial

How is the work priced?

+

Fees depend on scope; we confirm them in writing before work starts. Work is priced as a fixed fee for a defined deliverable or a day rate for ongoing support. Our working terms are published on How we work.

Do you offer payment plans or flexible billing?

+

Yes, we can work with most organisations on flexible billing arrangements. Whether you prefer monthly invoicing, milestone-based payments, or other structures, we're happy to discuss options that suit your cash flow.

What is your typical response time?

+

We reply within one working day. During an engagement, correspondence is also answered within one working day.

How do I get started with Planned Limited?

+

Use the contact form, email info@plannedltd.co.uk or call 020 4582 4620. We reply within one working day, agree the scope with you and send a written proposal. There is no obligation.
