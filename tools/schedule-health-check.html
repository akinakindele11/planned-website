Accepts .xer (P6 export) · .xml (P6 XML or Microsoft Project XML)
🔒 Your schedule never leaves your browser. The file is read and analysed on your own device and is never uploaded to any server. We count anonymous usage events so we know the tool is being used — your schedule data is never among them.

Using .mpp or Asta .pp? In Microsoft Project choose File → Save As → XML and upload the .xml. In Asta Powerproject, export to P6 XML. Binary formats can't be read in a browser — that's the cost of never uploading your file.

See how your grade compares

You already have your report — it downloaded to your device. What we can send you is the part you can't get from your own schedule: the anonymised UK average for each of the fourteen checks, and where your grade sits against every other programme run through this tool.

We store your email address and your grade letter. Nothing from your schedule is transmitted — not activity names, not dates, not the file.

Method. Checks follow the Defense Contract Management Agency 14-point schedule assessment. Percentages are computed over incomplete activities, excluding summary/WBS rows, level-of-effort and milestones where the check does not apply. Working-day conversions use the schedule's own hours-per-day where the file states it, otherwise 8.
