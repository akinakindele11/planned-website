Case Studies

Programme, assurance and Primavera work on energy and infrastructure, with teams in the UK and EMEA.

Selected work

Energy & Utilities

Enterprise P6 Transformation & Database Consolidation: UK Electricity Transmission

The challenge. A major UK electricity transmission operator was running its capital delivery portfolio across a large estate of separate Primavera P6 databases, with data accuracy far below the level portfolio reporting needs. Fragmented structures and no single source of truth made reliable portfolio reporting almost impossible.

What we did. We led the full consolidation, upgrade and cloud migration into a single enterprise P6 environment. That meant standardising the work breakdown structure, activity coding, calendars and layouts across the estate, cleansing and migrating the data, and putting proper governance around how schedules are built and maintained.

Role: P6 consolidation, upgrade and migration lead · Setting: UK electricity transmission capital portfolio · Tools: Primavera P6 (enterprise)

Energy & Utilities

Automated Earned Value Management Reporting: UK Electricity Transmission

The challenge. A UK electricity transmission operator's monthly earned value reporting was heavily manual and resource-hungry, and errors were slipping through into senior reporting.

What we did. We designed and built automated data validation and migration pipelines that fed clean outputs straight into senior management reporting, removing the manual re-keying that caused the errors.

Role: Earned value reporting and data automation · Setting: UK electricity transmission · Tools: Primavera P6, earned value management

Energy & Infrastructure

Offshore HVDC Interconnector Integrated Master Schedule

The challenge. A major subsea HVDC interconnector had to be planned across marine engineering, regulatory and infrastructure disciplines, and across international boundaries.

What we did. We built and maintained the Integrated Master Schedule in P6, coordinating every discipline into one coherent programme, running full critical path and sensitivity analysis, and integrating earned value so leadership could see progress and exposure clearly.

Role: Integrated Master Schedule planner · Setting: subsea HVDC interconnector · Tools: Primavera P6, critical path and sensitivity analysis, earned value

Highways & Infrastructure

P6 Implementation & Schedule Governance: National Roads Authority

The challenge. A UK national roads authority's Major Projects Directorate needed consistent Primavera P6 implementation and disciplined supplier programme governance, including NEC Clause 31 and 32 compliance, across a large planning community.

What we did. We led the enterprise P6 implementation across the directorate, built the supplier programme submission process to meet NEC requirements, and embedded planning governance and best practice across the teams and their supply chain.

Role: P6 implementation and supplier programme governance lead · Contract: NEC, clauses 31 and 32 · Tools: Primavera P6

Gas Distribution

Oracle Primavera Cloud Enterprise Implementation

The challenge. A major gas distribution network needed enterprise scheduling that could flag delay early and feed regulatory reporting, rather than a set of disconnected schedules.

What we did. We led the enterprise-wide Oracle Primavera Cloud and P6 implementation, built diagnostic tools that surface delay before it bites, and integrated schedule, cost and risk data so regulatory reporting drew from one consistent picture.

Role: Oracle Primavera Cloud and P6 implementation lead · Setting: gas distribution network · Tools: Oracle Primavera Cloud, Primavera P6

Cross-Industry

P6 & OPC Consultancy: Aerospace, Rail & Nuclear

The challenge. Organisations across aerospace, rail and nuclear, three demanding and heavily regulated sectors, needed robust Primavera environments and reporting they could trust.

What we did. Acting as principal consultant across several engagements, we designed P6 environments, configured Oracle Primavera Cloud, built governance frameworks, and delivered Power BI dashboards that turned schedule data into something leadership could actually use.

Role: Principal consultant · Sectors: aerospace, rail and nuclear · Tools: Primavera P6, Oracle Primavera Cloud, Power BI

Telecoms

Regional Network Rollout Programme

The challenge. A national telecoms provider's Midlands network rollout needed resource- and cost-loaded programmes and a regional scheduling team capable of running them.

What we did. As regional scheduling lead, we built end-to-end cost-loaded programmes, managed the critical path activities that drove delivery, and mentored the schedulers across the region.

Role: Regional scheduling lead · Setting: regional telecoms network rollout · Tools: cost and resource loaded programmes

Professional Services

OPC Schedule Management & Team Enablement

The challenge. A professional-services organisation wanted Oracle Primavera Cloud stood up properly and its team able to run it without leaning on outside help indefinitely.

What we did. We configured OPC from scratch, covering project codes, resources, templates and dashboards, then revised the existing programme plan, provided sustained ongoing support, and delivered tailored training to build genuine internal capability.

Role: Configuration, support and training · Setting: professional services · Tools: Oracle Primavera Cloud

Architecture & Design, Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia: Design Management Programme Scheduling (Cultural Giga-Project)

The challenge. A major Saudi Arabian cultural destination giga-project demanded an integrated design programme that met the developer's PMO reporting standards at every gateway, across many specialist disciplines.

What we did. Embedded with a UK design management consultancy, we built an integrated design programme in P6 spanning architecture, landscape, lighting and specialist disciplines, and aligned it to the developer's PMO standards so every gateway review had the evidence it needed.

Role: Design programme planner, embedded with a UK design management consultancy · Setting: Saudi Arabia, developer PMO standards · Tools: Primavera P6
