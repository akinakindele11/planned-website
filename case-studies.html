Energy & Utilities

Enterprise P6 Transformation & Database Consolidation: UK Electricity Transmission

The challenge. A major UK electricity transmission operator was running its capital delivery portfolio across 31 separate Primavera P6 databases, with data accuracy at around 23%. Fragmented structures and no single source of truth made reliable portfolio reporting almost impossible.

What we did. We led the full consolidation, upgrade and cloud migration into a single enterprise P6 environment. That meant standardising the work breakdown structure, activity coding, calendars and layouts across the estate, cleansing and migrating the data, and putting proper governance around how schedules are built and maintained.

The result. Thirty-one databases became one, data accuracy rose from 23% to 99.7%, and the business finally had a trustworthy, standardised single source for portfolio reporting.

Automated Earned Value Management Reporting: UK Electricity Transmission

Offshore HVDC Interconnector Integrated Master Schedule: UK–Europe

The challenge. A multi-billion-pound subsea HVDC interconnector linking the UK with European partners had to be planned across marine engineering, regulatory and infrastructure disciplines, and across international boundaries.

What we did. We built and maintained the Integrated Master Schedule in P6, coordinating every discipline into one coherent programme, running full critical path and sensitivity analysis, and integrating earned value so leadership could see progress and exposure clearly.

The result. A single multi-discipline master schedule, full critical-path visibility, and EVM and sensitivity reporting that stood up to the scrutiny a programme of that scale attracts.

Highways & Infrastructure

P6 Implementation & Schedule Governance: National Roads Authority

The challenge. A UK national roads authority's Major Projects Directorate needed consistent Primavera P6 implementation and disciplined supplier programme governance, including NEC Clause 31 and 32 compliance, across a large planning community.

What we did. We led the enterprise P6 implementation across the directorate, built the supplier programme submission process to meet NEC requirements, and embedded planning governance and best practice across the teams and their supply chain.

The result. Enterprise P6 live across the directorate, a working NEC-compliant programme submission process, and consistent planning governance where it had previously been patchy.
