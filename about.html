About Planned

A project planning and controls consultancy for construction and infrastructure.

Who we are

Planned Ltd plans, controls and checks programmes for contractors, subcontractors, consultancies and clients delivering construction and infrastructure projects. We work in Primavera P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud across construction, energy and utilities, and transport and infrastructure in the UK, and on Gulf projects under FIDIC.

We are independent: we are not the contractor and we do not sell software, so you get a straight view of what a programme can and cannot show.

How we are organised

Planned is led by Akingbenga Akindele Ibiyode, Director.

Each engagement has a named lead from our delivery team, agreed with you in the proposal. We draw on a bench of senior planners and specialists who work to the same standards, so we can add capacity when a job needs it. Every deliverable is reviewed by a senior planner before it is issued.

Planners interested in associate work can email a CV to akin@plannedltd.co.uk with ASSOCIATE in the subject line.

What we do

  • Planning and scheduling: tender programmes, NEC first programmes and monthly updates, MS Project to P6 conversion, and interim and retained planners.
  • Project controls and PMO support: cost and schedule control, reporting and earned value, risk analysis and resource management.
  • Delay and claims: extension of time and delay analysis, impacted programmes for compensation events and claims support. See the Delay and EOT position review.
  • Schedule assurance and reviews: NEC4 programme acceptance reviews, independent schedule assurance and migration readiness assessments.
  • Systems and training: P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud administration, training and the Planned Academy.
