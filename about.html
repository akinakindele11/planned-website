About Planned

A project planning and controls consultancy for construction and infrastructure.

Who we are

Planned Ltd plans, controls and checks programmes for contractors, consultancies and clients delivering construction and infrastructure projects. We work across construction, energy and utilities, transport and infrastructure, and defence, with teams in the UK and EMEA, using Primavera P6, Oracle Primavera Cloud and Microsoft Project.

We are independent. We are not the contractor and we do not sell software, so you get a straight view of what a programme can and cannot show.

Who does the work

Planned is led by Akingbenga Akindele Ibiyode, Director.

Each engagement has a named lead from our delivery team, agreed with you in the proposal. We draw on a bench of senior planners and specialists who work to the same standards, so we can add capacity when a job needs it. Every deliverable is reviewed by a senior planner before it is issued.

What we do

We build programmes for tenders and first programmes for acceptance under NEC, keep them updated through delivery, convert Microsoft Project programmes to Primavera P6, and provide planners for a project or a few days a month.

We support project teams with cost and schedule control, earned value and reporting, schedule risk analysis and resource planning.

When a project runs late, we assess the delay, prepare impacted programmes for compensation events and support extension of time claims. If you are not sure where you stand, the Delay and EOT Position Review is a good place to start.

We review programmes independently, including NEC4 acceptance reviews and schedule assurance, and we set up and administer Primavera P6 and Oracle Primavera Cloud and train the people who use them.
